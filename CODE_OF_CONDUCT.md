# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. People with different backgrounds,
experience levels, and opinions should be able to participate in crewboss.

- Discuss the work and the evidence. Critique ideas without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and respect a contributor's time and boundaries.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or post sexual content or personal
  information without consent.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant; do not spam or repeatedly derail a
  discussion.

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, and other
project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content,
lock discussions, or restrict participation. They should explain moderation
decisions when doing so will not expose private information.

To raise a concern, contact a repository maintainer through an available private
channel. If you do not have one, open an issue asking for a private contact
without naming people or sharing sensitive details. Do not post private reports
in public threads.
